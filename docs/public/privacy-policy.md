# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** [Date]

FreeBNB ("we," "our," or "us") lets people offer their home to friends when they travel. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using FreeBNB, you agree to the practices described here.

If you have questions, please contact **theworkingcell@gmail.com**.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, information we collect automatically, and information from third parties.

**1.1 Information You Provide to Us:**
- **Account information:** When you sign up, we collect the information provided by your chosen sign-in method (Sign in with Apple, Google, or email/password), including your name, email address, and a unique account identifier. You may also browse the app anonymously as a guest before creating an account.
- **Profile information:** Information you choose to add to your profile, such as a photo, bio, and preferences.
- **Listings:** If you host, we collect the details you provide about your space: address, amenities, capacity, availability, house preferences, and any co-hosts you add. Listing addresses are converted to map coordinates on your device; **FreeBNB does not access your device's precise location data.**
- **Friend connections:** Information about who you've connected with as friends, since friend connections determine which listings you can see.
- **Messages & Stay requests:** Content of messages you send through the app's in-app chat, and dates, listing, and status information for stay requests.
- **Reviews and references:** Public reviews, private notes, and friend-written references you write about other users, and those written about you.
- **Reports and safety information:** Details of reports you submit about another user, and emergency contact information if provided for a stay.

**1.2 Information We Collect Automatically:**
- **Usage and device information:** Basic technical information needed to operate the app, such as push notification tokens, IP addresses, device types, operating systems, and information generated through your use of the app (for example, saved listings or crash data).

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Operate core features: listings, stay requests, messaging, friend connections, reviews, and references.
- **Enforce the friends-only visibility model**, so listings are only shown to a host's accepted friends.
- Send push notifications related to messages, stay requests, and account activity.
- Detect and prevent abuse, including automated content moderation, message rate limiting, and enforcement of blocks.
- Respond to reports and maintain a moderation queue for safety review.
- Maintain, secure, and improve the app.
- Comply with legal obligations.

**We do not sell your personal information.**

## 3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Compliant)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the UK, we process your personal data under the following legal bases:
- **Contractual Necessity:** To perform our contract with you (e.g., creating your account, facilitating connections for stays).
- **Legitimate Interests:** To improve the Platform, ensure security, and prevent fraud, provided those interests do not outweigh your fundamental rights.
- **Legal Obligation:** To comply with applicable laws.

## 4. How Information Is Shared

**4.1 With other users, by design:**
FreeBNB is built around visibility to people you know. Your listings are visible to your accepted friends. Messages are visible to the other party in that conversation. Reviews you write publicly are visible to other users; private notes and references are visible only to the intended recipient. Friends of friends only see suggestions that you exist as a possible connection, not your listings or profile details.

**4.2 With service providers:**
We use Firebase (a Google service) for authentication, data storage, backend functions, and push notifications. These providers process data on our behalf and are bound by their own data protection obligations.

**4.3 For safety and legal reasons:**
We may access or disclose information if required by law, or if we believe in good faith it's necessary to investigate a report, prevent harm, enforce our Terms of Service, or defend against legal claims.

**We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.**

## 5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. Deleted listings are soft-deleted (marked as removed but not immediately erased) to preserve the history of past stays and conversations for the other party involved.

If you delete your account, we permanently remove your account data and cascade the deletion across associated records (listings, messages, stay requests, friend connections) as described in Section 7.

## 6. Security

We rely on Firebase Authentication and Firestore security rules as the primary technical safeguards for your data. Access to listings, messages, and profile data is enforced server-side based on friendship status and blocking, not just hidden in the app's interface. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

## 7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live (including California, the EEA, and the UK), you may have specific rights regarding your personal data:

- **Export your data:** You can request an export of the data associated with your account from within the app.
- **Delete your account:** You can delete your account from within the app. Deleting your account removes your profile and cascades deletion across your listings, messages, stay requests, and friend connections tied to your account.
- **Blocking:** You can block other users. Blocking is enforced at the data-access level, not just in the interface.
- **Manage notifications:** You can control push notification permissions through your device's system settings.
- **Right to Access/Rectification/Erasure:** You may have the right to access, correct, or restrict processing of your data, or request deletion (Right to be Forgotten).

**California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA):**
While we do not sell data to brokers, if any third-party analytics sharing qualifies as a "sale" or "sharing" under California law, you have the right to opt-out. You also have the right to limit the use of your sensitive personal information.

Contact us at **theworkingcell@gmail.com** to exercise these rights. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

## 8. Age Requirement

FreeBNB is intended for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to use the app. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have collected information from someone under 18, we will delete it.
